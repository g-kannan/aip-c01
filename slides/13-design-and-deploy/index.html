01 / Requirements

Start from the need, not the model.

VALUE

The outcome

What decision or task improves, and how will you measure it?

CONSTRAINTS

The limits

Latency, budget, data residency, accuracy, who may see what.

RISK

The downside

Harm, compliance, and what a wrong answer costs.

SHAPE

The traffic

Steady, spiky, batch; users or systems; peak and idle.

Write these down first. They decide every later choice.

03 / Well-Architected

Six pillars, six GenAI questions.

PillarAsk
Operational excellenceAre prompts versioned and answers traceable?
SecurityAre inputs and outputs guarded, and access least-privilege?
ReliabilityWhat happens on throttling, an outage, a bad answer?
Performance efficiencyIs the model the smallest that meets quality and latency?
Cost optimizationAre tokens, caching and model tiers managed?
SustainabilityIs capacity right-sized, with idle compute minimized?

05 / SageMaker shapes

Same platform, different workload shapes.

REAL-TIME

Inference components

Several warm models share one endpoint's GPUs and scale independently.

ASYNC

Queue and scale to zero

Long or bursty requests: the queue buffers, and idle cost drops to zero.

BATCH

Batch transform

Score a whole dataset offline, then release the instances.

Count GPU memory, load time and idle hours before picking real-time.

Large models need GPU memory for weights plus the context cache, and take time to load, which makes scale-to-zero a trade-off against cold starts. GPU endpoints are expensive: tear them down after practice.
